How ordering food with a real AI agent works

“Order my usual Thai curry,” or “Get groceries for tacos tonight.” Super understands intent, not rigid commands.

2. Super uses the computer

Unlike chat-only assistants like Siri, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok, Super can actually open sites, navigate menus, and fill carts.

Super shows you what it prepared and waits for confirmation, keeping you in control.
